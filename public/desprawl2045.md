### De-sprawl 2045

The maps show modelled results at hectare resolution for all of Switzerland. Colored hectares represent areas retained and renovated as future 8-minute neighborhoods. Their color indicates the accessibility score: higher scores mean that essential daily services are closer and easier to reach on foot. The service layers show the modelled placement of services such as grocery stores, schools, healthcare, childcare, and other everyday services.

Areas without colored hectares are not selected for long-term settlement in the scenario. Their population is reassigned to nearby retained areas. The results show how much built-up area, floor-space demand, service accessibility, and urban sprawl could change under this transformation pathway.

The maps aim to facilitate dialogue around housing, mobility, and wellbeing, and inform future deliberative democratic planning.
